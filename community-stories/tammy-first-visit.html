Tammy’s Visit to Versailles UMC

We were grateful to welcome Tammy to Versailles United Methodist Church. After worship, she stayed for our church picnic and later shared about her experience on Facebook after checking in and mentioning VUMC.

Moments like this remind us that hospitality is more than a greeting at the door. It is the way people are seen, welcomed, included, and invited into community.

Real stories like Tammy’s help show what it means to Love Like Jesus in everyday ways.

Whether you are visiting for the first time, returning to church after a long season, or looking for a church home in Versailles, Kentucky, we would be honored to welcome you.
