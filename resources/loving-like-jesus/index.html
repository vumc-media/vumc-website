About This Series

Loving Like Jesus is a devotional series from Versailles United Methodist Church exploring what it means to follow Jesus in everyday life. Through scripture, reflection, prayer, and practical application, each article encourages readers to grow in faith and discover new ways to love God and neighbor.

In a world full of noise, division, and uncertainty, Jesus continues to call us to a different way of living. Loving like Jesus is more than a slogan. It is a way of seeing people, serving others, practicing grace, and allowing Christ to shape our hearts.
