Serve at VUMC

Use your gifts. Make a difference. Love like Jesus.

There are many ways to serve at Versailles United Methodist Church — from hospitality and worship to local outreach, care ministries, and community support. Whether you want to serve inside the church or out in the community, there is a place for you.

Flexible Opportunities
Local Outreach

Serve our community in practical ways.

Local outreach ministries help us care for neighbors in tangible ways through food, compassion, encouragement, and presence.

3rd Monday Community Meal

Partnering with other local churches to prepare and deliver meals to neighbors in need on the third Monday of each month.

Blessing Box

A simple way to provide food and essential items for individuals and families facing hardship through a local compassion resource.

Backpack Buddies

Helping provide weekend food for children facing food insecurity while also reminding families that they are seen and cared for.

Diaper Bank

Providing diapers and practical support for families while meeting a real and ongoing need in the community.

Jail Ministry

Offering spiritual support, prayer, and meaningful connection to individuals who are incarcerated through God’s hope and grace.

Community Outreach Events

Serving through special outreach efforts, donation drives, and community gatherings where practical help and hospitality matter.

Care Ministries

Serving through presence, compassion, and relationship.

Some of the most meaningful ministry happens through simple presence — showing up, listening, praying, visiting, and reminding people they are not alone.

These opportunities may include nursing home visits, care-focused outreach, relationship-based encouragement, and ministries that extend the church’s care beyond Sunday morning.

If you have a heart for people, hospitality, compassion, or encouragement, there may be a place for you here.
