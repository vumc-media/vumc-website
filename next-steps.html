Your Next Step Starts Here

No matter where you are in your faith journey—curious, returning, growing, or ready to go deeper—there is always a next step. At Versailles UMC, we believe faith is not meant to be walked alone. We are here to help you discover meaningful ways to connect, grow, and serve.

Maybe you are exploring baptism, wondering about membership, or simply want to meet someone over coffee and ask questions. Whatever brings you here, you are welcome.

Coffee with the Pastor

Sometimes the best place to begin is a simple conversation. Meeting for coffee is a relaxed way to ask questions, share your story, and learn more about who we are as a church family.

Baptism

Baptism is a powerful sign of God’s grace and a meaningful step in your faith journey. Whether you are exploring baptism for yourself or for your child, we would love to walk alongside you and help you understand what it means.

Becoming a Member

Membership at Versailles UMC is about belonging to a community that supports one another in faith, service, and love. If you are ready to call this your church home, we will guide you through the process step by step.

Serving on a Team

Every person has gifts that matter. From worship and media to hospitality, children’s ministry, outreach, and more—there are many ways to serve and make a difference. Let us help you find a place where your passions meet purpose.

What Happens Next?

  • You fill out the form.
  • A staff member will reach out personally.
  • We will help you explore your interests.
  • You choose your pace—no pressure, just partnership.

Tap the button below to complete the Next Steps form and someone from our team will follow up personally. There is no pressure—just an invitation to take a step forward at your own pace.
