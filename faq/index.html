Questions? We’re Glad You Asked.

Whether you’re new to church, returning after a long time, or just learning more about VUMC, this page is here to help you feel more at home.

Welcome to Versailles United Methodist Church

We know visiting a church can feel like a big step. You may wonder where to go, what to wear, what worship is like, or whether there is a place for you. At VUMC, our heart is simple: we want to Love Like Jesus and help people take their next step with God.

We currently offer Traditional Worship at 9:30 AM and Contemporary Worship at 10:30 AM each Sunday.

Traditional Worship usually includes hymns, liturgy, and a more classic worship style. Contemporary Worship has a more modern feel with praise music and a relaxed atmosphere. Both services share the same heart: worshiping Jesus and growing in faith together.

Come as you are. Some people wear jeans, some dress up, and many are somewhere in between. You do not need to look a certain way to be welcomed here.

When you arrive, look for the main entrance and someone will be glad to help you find worship, children’s ministry, restrooms, or a place to sit. We want your first visit to feel easy, not overwhelming.

“Love Like Jesus” is more than a slogan for us. It is a way of living. It means showing grace, serving others, welcoming people, and reflecting the heart of Christ in our church and community.

Yes. Versailles United Methodist Church is part of the United Methodist Church. We are rooted in Christian faith, Wesleyan tradition, worship, service, discipleship, and community.

You can get connected by attending worship, filling out a connection form, joining a group, serving in a ministry, or reaching out to the church office. We would love to help you take your next step.

You can give online through Church Center or during worship. Giving supports the mission and ministry of VUMC as we worship, serve, care, and reach our community with the love of Jesus.
