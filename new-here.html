Questions people often have

If you are unsure where to begin, these are some of the most common things guests want to know.

What should I wear?Whatever feels comfortable. We want you to come as you are.
Do I need to know anyone first?No. You are welcome whether you know someone here or not.
Can I visit online first?Yes. Our watch page is a great way to explore before attending in person.

Want a simple first step?

You do not need to do everything at once. A first visit, an online service, or a quick look at the kids page may be all you need right now.

Visit in personStart with a Sunday morning and let the rest unfold naturally.
