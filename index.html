A place to encounter grace, truth, and community.

Church in Versailles, KY

A local church helping people Love Like Jesus.

Versailles United Methodist Church is a welcoming United Methodist congregation serving Versailles, Woodford County, and the surrounding Central Kentucky area. We gather for traditional and contemporary worship, offer online worship for those who are traveling or homebound, and create simple next steps for people to connect, pray, serve, and grow in faith.

Looking for a church near you?

If you are searching for a church in Versailles, KY, a United Methodist church in Central Kentucky, or a place where your family can worship and belong, we would love to welcome you. Our church family includes longtime members, new guests, online worshipers, children, students, adults, and people exploring faith for the first time.

Our mission is not just to host services, but to live out the gospel in everyday life. The phrase Love Like Jesus has become more than a hashtag for us. It is a reminder that worship should shape how we treat our neighbors, serve our community, care for one another, and point people toward Christ.

You can plan your first visit, watch worship online, explore next steps, or see upcoming church events anytime.
