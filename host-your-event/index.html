A Place to Gather, Celebrate, and Connect

Versailles United Methodist Church offers a variety of spaces that can serve families, ministries, community groups, and organizations. Whether you are planning a reception, meeting, shower, class, meal, or special event, our facility provides flexible options in a welcoming church environment.

Submit a Request
