VUMC Kids

VUMC Kids Children’s Ministry in Versailles KY

VUMC Kids is a joyful place for children to grow in faith and discover the love of God in a safe, welcoming, and engaging environment. Through Bible teaching, worship, creativity, and caring relationships, we want every child to know they are loved and that they belong here.

Our goal is to create an environment where children can learn about Jesus, build friendships, and begin forming a faith that grows with them.

Why VUMC Kids?

Helping children build a strong beginning in faith.

Childhood is a powerful season for learning, trust, wonder, and belonging. We want VUMC Kids to be a place where children can ask questions, experience joy, and begin learning the story of God’s love in ways they can understand.

What makes this ministry special

We want kids to feel safe, seen, and excited to come back each week.

Faith made understandable Bible truths are presented in ways children can connect with and remember.
Learning through creativity Music, crafts, movement, and interaction help children stay engaged.
A welcoming environment Caring volunteers help every child feel included, loved, and valued.

Good to know

Families often want to know what to expect before the first visit.

Children are welcome here We want families to feel comfortable taking a first step without pressure.
You do not need to know everything first We’re happy to help families get oriented when they arrive.
Every child matters Our heart is to help children experience faith, joy, and belonging.
A Place to Grow

Faith that starts early can shape a lifetime.

Planting seeds of faith in young hearts.

VUMC Kids is designed to help children grow in their love for God in a safe, fun, and engaging environment. Through age-appropriate Bible lessons, music, crafts, and interactive activities, children learn foundational truths of faith while building friendships and having a meaningful experience at church.

Our caring volunteers are committed to helping every child feel welcomed, loved, and excited to learn more about Jesus.

For Families

You do not have to figure everything out before you come.

We want your family to feel at home.

Whether you have children who are excited, nervous, curious, or brand new to church life, we want families to know there is room for them here. VUMC Kids exists to support families as they help children begin learning what it means to know and follow Jesus.

If you are planning a first visit, our Kids & Families page is a great place to begin, and we would love to help you take the next step with confidence.
