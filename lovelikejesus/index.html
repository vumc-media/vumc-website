A Church Striving to Love Like Jesus

We believe Jesus calls us to love God, love our neighbors, serve with compassion, extend grace, seek truth, and walk humbly together.

What does it mean to Love Like Jesus?

Love Like Jesus means we are learning to follow Christ in practical, everyday ways. It shapes how we worship, how we welcome, how we serve, how we speak, and how we walk with people when life is messy, complicated, or uncomfortable.

Worship

We gather to encounter God, grow in faith, and remember that Jesus is at the center of all we do.

Welcome

We want people to know they are seen, valued, and invited to take their next step with Christ.

Serve

We love our community through compassion, mission, generosity, and practical acts of care.

Grow

We are not perfect people. We are people learning, day by day, how to follow Jesus faithfully.

Not just a hashtag. A way of life.

#LoveLikeJesus is our reminder that faith is not only something we believe. It is something we live.

The heart behind the phrase

At VUMC, Love Like Jesus does not mean we have all the answers or that we always get everything right. It means we are committed to becoming a church where people encounter the grace, truth, compassion, and hope of Christ.

We believe the Church is not simply a building. The Church is a community of people called to live out God’s love in the world.

What shapes this calling?

Our mission is rooted in the historic Christian faith, centered on Jesus Christ, and lived out through loving God and loving our neighbors.

God

We believe God is the Creator, Savior, and Spirit — present with us, working among us, and calling us into a life of love, worship, and service.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is Lord. Not our politics. Not our preferences. Not our comfort. Jesus shows us what love looks like through grace, truth, sacrifice, and resurrection life.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit empowers ordinary people for extraordinary purposes, giving strength, wisdom, comfort, and courage to live as followers of Christ.

The Church

We believe God moves through people. Together we worship, grow in faith, serve our neighbors, and participate in God’s mission in the world.

God’s Kingdom

We believe God’s Kingdom is both present and coming. Wherever love triumphs over hate, mercy meets need, and grace makes room at the table, the Kingdom of God is breaking in.

The Bible

We believe Scripture helps tell the story of God’s relationship with humanity and points us to the Living Word, Jesus Christ.

Love God. Love People. Love Like Jesus.

This is our mission, our witness, and our prayer. Whether you are exploring faith, returning to church, or looking for a church home, there is a place for you here.
