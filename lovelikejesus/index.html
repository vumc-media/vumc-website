Love Like Jesus

At Versailles United Methodist Church, Love Like Jesus is more than a phrase. It is becoming the heartbeat of who we are and who we are called to be.

What Does That Mean? Our Mission

A Church Striving to Love Like Jesus

We believe Jesus calls us to love God, love our neighbors, serve with compassion, extend grace, seek truth, and walk humbly together.

What does it mean to Love Like Jesus?

Love Like Jesus means we are learning to follow Christ in practical, everyday ways. It shapes how we worship, how we welcome, how we serve, how we speak, and how we walk with people when life is messy, complicated, or uncomfortable.

Worship

We gather to encounter God and grow in faith.

Welcome

We want people to know they belong.

Serve

We love our community through action.

Grow

We are learning to follow Jesus daily.

What shapes this calling?

Our mission is rooted in the historic Christian faith, centered on Jesus Christ, and lived out through loving God and loving our neighbors.

God

We believe God is the Creator, Savior, and Spirit — present with us, working among us, and calling us into a life of love.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is Lord. Not our politics. Not our preferences. Jesus shows us what love looks like.

The Holy Spirit

The Spirit empowers ordinary people for extraordinary purposes.

The Church

The Church is a community of people called to live out God’s love.

Love God. Love People. Love Like Jesus.
