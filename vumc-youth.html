Rooted Youth Ministry

Growing Deep. Living Strong.

Rooted Youth Ministry is a place where middle and high school students come together to grow in faith, build friendships, and discover their purpose in Christ.

We meet every Sunday at 4:00 PM for a time of worship, Bible teaching, games, and small group discussion in a fun and welcoming environment.

Whether you’re new to church or have grown up in it, Rooted is a place where you can be real, be known, and be encouraged.

Why this matters

The teenage years are full of change, questions, identity, pressure, and growth. We want students to know they do not have to navigate those years alone.

Faith that connects to real lifeStudents need more than information. They need a faith that can meet them where they are.
Friendships that matterYouth ministry creates space for students to belong, connect, and build meaningful relationships.
A place to be encouragedVUMC Youth is meant to be a place where students are seen, known, and reminded that they matter to God.

What families should know

It can help to know a few simple things before taking a first step.

Students do not have to know anyone firstNew students are welcome, and we want them to feel comfortable getting connected.
Questions are okayYouth need room to ask honest questions and explore faith in a real and encouraging environment.
The focus is both faith and belongingWe want students to grow spiritually while also experiencing friendship, support, and community.
First Time?

You do not have to figure everything out before coming.

A first step can be simple.

If your student is looking for a place to connect, ask questions, and grow, VUMC Youth can be a meaningful starting point. Whether they are excited, cautious, or somewhere in between, we would love to help them feel at home.

If your family is planning a first visit, the Kids & Families page is a great place to begin, and we would love to help you take that first step with confidence.
