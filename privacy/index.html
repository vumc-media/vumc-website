Privacy Policy

VUMC Communications

Our commitment to privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how Versailles United Methodist Church uses information within VUMC Communications and related internal staff tools.

About this application

VUMC Communications is an internal ministry communications tool used by authorized church staff and approved ministry leaders. It supports church announcements, email communication, Facebook Page publishing, and internal recordkeeping.

Information we may process

The application may process limited information needed to complete church communication tasks, including:

  • Names and email addresses stored in church-managed contact lists.
  • Announcement titles and message content.
  • Public webpage links submitted for sharing.
  • Images selected by authorized users for Facebook Page posts.
  • Basic publishing records, such as date, selected audience, and delivery status.

How information is used

Information is used only for legitimate church and ministry purposes, including:

  • Sending church announcements and ministry updates.
  • Publishing approved content to the church Facebook Page.
  • Maintaining an internal archive of communications.
  • Supporting church administration, care, connection, and ministry coordination.

Third-party services

VUMC Communications uses trusted third-party services to perform authorized functions. These may include Google, Meta/Facebook, Supabase, and GitHub.

Information may be transmitted to these services only when needed to complete a requested action, such as sending an email or publishing a Facebook Page post.

Data sharing and sale

Versailles United Methodist Church does not sell personal information. Information is not shared for advertising, marketing resale, or unrelated commercial purposes.

Access and security

Access to VUMC Communications is restricted to authorized users. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect accounts, application access, and church-managed information.

Authorized users are expected to protect their sign-in credentials and use the application only for approved church purposes.

Data retention

Communication records may be retained for ministry, administrative, historical, and accountability purposes. Information may be removed when it is no longer needed or when removal is appropriate.

Requests and questions

Questions about this policy, access to information, or requests for correction or deletion may be directed to Versailles United Methodist Church.
